# Source facts and publication boundary

Use selected factual numerical cells from NHTSA's official State Traffic Data reports (2019, 2021, 2023): state-level FARS fatalities and rounded person-type shares; Census Bureau population and FHWA licensed-driver/VMT figures as compiled and attributed in those reports. This is a public aggregate statistical panel, not person-level fatality, clinical, address, crash-location or VIN data.

Complete responses, decoded PDFs and rendered source pages stay outside Git. Publish only selected factual table cells, exact source locators/hashes and original analysis/figures. Do not publish third-party papers, report prose, scanned tables or agency logos. Do not claim a blanket CC 0 license for the source publications. The U.S. Copyright Office distinguishes unprotected facts from protected expression; source authors/compilers are credited and no endorsement is implied.

Registered-vehicle fields/rates are excluded, including national registration figures explicitly attributed to copyrighted R.L.Polk/S&P Global Mobility material. National denominators from different compilers are not assumed interchangeable or additive; retain reconciliation differences for the included government denominators.

The reviewed independent manuscript is authorized after checks. Separate formal-publication projects, including Azure optimization, remain private.
